# Risk Register

> Records project risks and countermeasures; review periodically and update status. Add a new row whenever a new risk is found.

> ⚠️ example — delete when starting real content: R-001 / R-002 below are format demonstrations.

| ID | Risk description | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Countermeasure / Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|----|----------|------|--------|------|-------------|--------|------|
| R-001 | Example: shortage of a critical component | Supply chain | Medium | High | Find a second supplier, keep buffer stock |  | Open |
| R-002 | Example: frequent contract changes cause rework | Requirements | Medium | Medium | Thoroughly review contracts before finalizing, follow change management |  | Open |

> **Category** (reference): technical, supply chain, requirements, schedule, security, regulatory, staffing.
> **Likelihood / Impact**: High / Medium / Low.
> **Status**: Open | Monitoring | Mitigated | Closed.
